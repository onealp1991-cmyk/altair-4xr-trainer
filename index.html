ALTAIR 4XR Trainer

Press the power button to start. The unit runs the real startup sequence (timed from MSA's own video), then flashes FAS? for 10 seconds.

Buttons in normal mode

  • ⏻ confirm. Hold 5 s to power off.
  • ▼ next page: BUMP TEST? → PEAK → MIN → STEL → TWA → TIME → DATE → dSCVRY → MOTION. Hold 3 s for InstantAlert.
  • ▲ reset the value on PEAK / MIN / STEL / TWA pages, acknowledge or silence alarms. Hold 3 s to enter calibration (ZERO CAL? then SPAN CAL?).

Bump test

▼ once to bUMP TEST?, then ⏻. The sim applies cal gas for you (58 % LEL, 15.0 % O2, 60 ppm CO, 20 ppm H2S). The readings climb, then bUMP PASS and the ✓ stays on the main page for 24 h. ▲ backs out.

Calibration

Hold ▲ 3 s for ZERO CAL?. ⏻ runs SENSOR REFRSH, zeroes in fresh air (ZERO PASS), then SPAN CAL? alternates with the cal-gas values. ⏻ spans with gas (SPAN PASS) and the LAST CAL date updates. ▼ skips either step. 30 s of no input returns to normal.

Classroom scenarios

The Scenario button (bottom left) cycles through five atmospheres. Each names the known gas; the meter shows the reading. Students find the gas in the chart below and multiply: true % LEL = displayed % LEL × factor. Hold the Scenario button to reveal the answer. Tap it again after the last one to return to fresh air.

Conversion factors (manual section 5.2, page 47)

Multiply the displayed % LEL by the factor. Pentane column applies to units calibrated on the 58 % LEL pentane simulant (the default here); methane column for units calibrated to 33 % LEL methane. MSA notes individual units may vary by ±25 %.
